Sources & limitations

Public catalogs: Open Library, Google Books, Internet Archive. Search links also include Scribd and Google Scholar.

View counts are shown only when a source provides them or you import them. Internet Archive downloads are a separate metric, never views. This website does not scrape Scribd or bypass its restrictions. Results depend on public APIs and browser connectivity.
